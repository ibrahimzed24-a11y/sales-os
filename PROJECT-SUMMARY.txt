1. OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Send cold outreach to independent Canadian dental clinics and book free growth
audits for Valioz Health.

Target cadence:
  - Every Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday (active days only).
  - Exactly 10 clinics per active day.
  - Each email lands at 08:00 in the clinic's own Canadian time zone.
  - One personalized email per clinic, never a bulk blast.
  - Every clinic is used once; the tracker is the source of truth.

Offer: a free growth audit -> https://health.valioz.com/
Sender: sales@health.valioz.com (Hostinger webmail).


2. NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- 10 distinct clinics per day. Verified, uncontacted, non-DSO.
- Address the Principal Dentist or Practice Manager from real clinic data.
  Never invent a name, title, patient count, revenue figure or statistic.
- The clinic must be independent. A "parent_dso == Independent" flag is trusted
  but spot-checked; known DSO members are excluded by hand.
- Subject and body must be clinic-specific (clinic name, city, real site).
- Never offer "Your own custom website" to a clinic that already has a live
  website. That journey step is only for broken or unknown sites.
- "Book Your Free Growth Audit" always links to https://health.valioz.com/.
- Keep the unsubscribe link: https://health.valioz.com/unsubscribe/
- Keep the HIPAA notice and the WhatsApp link +20 10 93272247 ->
  https://wa.me/201093272247
- Do not use Hostinger's calendar or schedule-send (premium-gated). Scheduling
  is done locally, sending is done in the signed-in webmail session.


3. PIPELINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  research CSV  ->  lead scoring  ->  queue builder  ->  rendered HTML
        |                                                    |
        v                                                    v
  decision makers  -------------------------------->  manifest.json
                                                             |
                                                             v
                                      launchd (Tue/Wed/Thu 12:00 Cairo)
                                                             |
                                                             v
                                      Python runner + browser-harness
                                                             |
                                                             v
                                      Hostinger webmail (08:00 local)
                                                             |
                                                             v
                                      tracker.csv rows flip Pending -> Sent

Two clocks are involved:
  - launchd wakes the Mac once per active day at 12:00 Cairo.
  - The Python runner then sleeps until each clinic's exact UTC send time,
    which equals 08:00 in that clinic's province.


4. COMPONENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
scripts/outreach-queue.js
  Selects eligible leads, caps 3 per province, resolves decision makers,
  renders step-01 HTML, converts 08:00 local to UTC and Cairo, writes the
  per-day manifest and appends/updates the tracker.
  Flags: --date YYYY-MM-DD, --only slug-a,slug-b, --dry-run, --status

scripts/send-outreach.py
  Reads dist/outreach-queue/<date>/manifest.json, waits for each send time,
  opens Hostinger compose, commits the recipient chip, injects the HTML,
  clicks Send, waits for the "Message sent successfully" toast, then updates
  the tracker row to Sent. Retries Enter if the recipient chip has not
  resolved after ~3 seconds; refuses to click Send without a committed chip.

scripts/run-outreach.sh
  launchd entry point. cds into the runtime, runs caffeinate -i -s, then
  pipes send-outreach.py into browser-harness. Logs to
  dist/outreach-queue/scheduler.log

scripts/deploy-outreach-runtime.sh
  Mirrors sender, queue builder and manifests into
  ~/Library/Application Support/Valioz/outreach-runtime and hardlinks the
  tracker. Needed because macOS TCC blocks launchd from reading ~/Documents.

~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.valioz.outreach.plist
  Loaded agent. StartCalendarInterval: Weekday 2, 3, 4 at 12:00 local.
  Label com.valioz.outreach. ProcessType Interactive.

lib/sequence-content.js
  The five canonical sequence steps, subject builder, greeting fallback,
  unsubscribe URL. Steps:
    01-welcome, 02-pas-awareness, 03-aida-conversion, 04-case-study,
    05-urgency
  Daily cold outreach currently uses only 01-welcome.

lib/offer-content.js
  Shared offer block, CTAs, pricing, and the live-site vs no-site journey
  variant.

lib/valioz-brand.js
  Logo URL, sender identity, WhatsApp, Damietta address, brand tokens.

lib/builder.js, lib/template-engine.js, scripts/build-templates.js
  Dual-brand HTML assembly and template build.

scripts/validate-templates.js
  QA pass over rendered emails. Currently passes with 4 known warnings for
  empty resolved_website_url rows.

scripts/enrich-clinic-logos.js
  Downloads clinic logos/favicons, extracts accent colours, writes the
  enriched CSV.

scripts/sync-hostinger.js
  Stages templates into Hostinger (not used for the daily scheduled send).

config/30-day-plan.js
  LEGACY Mon/Wed/Fri plan. Not the active five-step sequence. Do not treat
  it as the current campaign.

7. SCHEDULING AND SEND RECIPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Send times (08:00 clinic-local -> UTC):
  AT  08:00 -> 12:00Z
  ET  08:00 -> 13:00Z
  CT  08:00 -> 14:00Z   (system tzdata 2026f)
  MT  08:00 -> 14:00Z   (Alberta on permanent UTC-6 in 2026f)
  PT  08:00 -> 15:00Z   (BC on permanent UTC-7 in 2026f)

launchd fires Tue/Wed/Thu at 12:00 Cairo, which is before every send.

Compose recipe used by the runner:
  1. Open a new Hostinger message.
  2. Set #to with the native value setter.
  3. Focus the field, wait ~3 s for the chip to resolve, press Enter.
  4. Refuse to continue until the address is committed as a chip.
  5. Set #subject.
  6. Inject the rendered HTML into
     iframe [data-qa="composer-content-body"].
  7. Real click_at_xy on Send.
  8. Wait for the "Message sent successfully" toast.
  9. Mark the tracker row Sent with both UTC and local timestamps.
